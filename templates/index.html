How to Play (4 Simple Steps)

1. Look at Clues

Click on rooms and look at the clues found by police. Click "Inspect" to see secret details.

2. Talk to Suspects

Ask the 4 suspects where they were and what they were doing at the time of the crime.

3. Catch Their Lies

Pick a clue that proves a suspect is lying, and click "Catch in a Lie!" to break their story.

4. Catch the Criminal

Click "Catch the Thief (Accuse)" at the top right, pick the criminal and the clue, and win the game!
